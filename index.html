How to build a 20-minute weekly SEO review routine for beginners

  1. Start with a 20-minute weekly review by checking your site's Search Console reports for critical errors and updates
  2. Use the URL Inspection tool to verify that your page content is visible to Google
  3. Focus on core SEO tasks that align with your business goals rather than overcomplicating the review
  4. Avoid asking for excessive reports that take time away from your SEO vendor's work
